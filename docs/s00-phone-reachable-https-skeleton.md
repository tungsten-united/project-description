# S00: Phone-reachable HTTPS skeleton

P0 | 20-30 min | Infrastructure owner (C) | Wave 1

- Depends on: nothing.
- Parallel with: S01.
- Unblocks: on-phone testing for S02 and S06; the endpoint used by S09.

Purpose: get an empty app onto the demo phone over HTTPS, because phone browsers only allow camera and microphone access on secure pages.

Acceptance: an empty app is served from a teammate's computer through the agreed tunnel; it loads on the demo phone over HTTPS; the camera and microphone permission prompts appear; the URL and restart steps are written down for the team.
